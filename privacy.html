Privacy Policy

MacMotion — Easy Video Editor for Mac
Effective date: 24 October 2026

In short: MacMotion does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. Your videos, photos, music and projects stay on your Mac. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

MacMotion is developed by Nikhil Fuge (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the MacMotion app for macOS (“the app”) handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send any information about you, your device or your use of the app to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not ask you to create an account.

Your media and projects

  • MacMotion only opens the videos, photos and audio files you choose to import, through the standard macOS file panels or by drag and drop.
  • All editing, previewing and exporting happens on your Mac. Your media is never uploaded.
  • Project files (.macmotion) are saved where you choose. They contain your edits and references to the location of your media files, so the app can find them again. They stay on your Mac unless you share them yourself.
  • The app keeps a few settings on your Mac, such as whether you have seen the welcome tour. These settings never leave your Mac.

In-App Purchase

MacMotion Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase processed entirely by Apple through the Mac App Store. We do not receive your name, email address, payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store, on your Mac, whether this Apple ID owns MacMotion Pro. Apple’s handling of purchase information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Crash reports and App Store statistics

If you have chosen in macOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage statistics. These do not identify you. You can change this at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Third parties

The app does not include any third-party SDKs and does not share information with third parties.

Children

MacMotion does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can remove all of the app’s data from your Mac by deleting the app, its projects and its settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We will not start collecting personal data without updating this policy first.
